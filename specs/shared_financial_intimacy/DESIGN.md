## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, sophisticated, and calm atmosphere designed specifically for couples navigating mutual financial health. The emotional response prioritizes psychological safety, transparency, partnership, and empowerment—removing the anxiety and friction frequently associated with shared budgeting, debt payoff, and wealth building.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Corporate Polish** with **Luminous Dark Mode Surfaces**:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Rich, obsidian and deep-slate bases create an intimate, focused canvas where financial figures don't feel aggressive or overwhelming.
- **Vibrant Optimism:** Luminous emerald tones serve as beacon points, symbolizing collaborative growth, portfolio vitality, and achieved shared milestones.
- **Tactile Clarity:** Soft luminescent outlines, subtle atmospheric backdrops, and glassy surface contrast lend tactile depth, framing shared goals as tangible, collaborative objects.

## Colors

The color palette is engineered for high legibility in low-light environments, relying on distinct tonal layers and vibrant semantic cues.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Accent (`#10B981` / Emerald 500, `#059669` / Emerald 600):** Anchors joint contributions, net positive balances, investment progress, and primary call-to-actions. Conveys security, alignment, and health.
- **Secondary Accent (`#38BDF8` / Sky 400):** Represents individual partner activity, comparative visual indicators, transfers, and real-time synchronizations.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#F59E0B` / Amber 500):** Designates shared limits, upcoming joint liabilities, and pending budget approvals between partners.
- **Surfaces & Neutral Bases:**
  - `Canvas / Root`: `#0B0F17` (Deep Obsidian Void)
  - `Surface Level 1 / Sectional`: `#111827` (Rich Night Slate)
  - `Surface Level 2 / Card Base`: `#182234` (Midnight Petrol Slate)
  - `Surface Level 3 / Active Surface`: `#1F2D42` (Elevated Interactive Slate)
  - `Surface Level 4 / Hover & Input`: `#26354D`
- **Borders & Dividers:**
  - `Structural Border`: `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.6)` (`#334155` at 60%)
  - `Subtle Divider`: `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.8)` (`#1E293B` at 80%)
  - `Active/Focus Glow Border`: `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.45)`
- **Typography & Content:**
  - `Pure High-Contrast Text`: `#F9FAFB` (Slate 50) for headers, critical values, and main titles.
  - `Muted Secondary Text`: `#94A3B8` (Slate 400) for labels, metadata, captions, and tooltips.
  - `Subtle Placeholder Text`: `#64748B` (Slate 500) for input prompts and inactive states.

## Typography

The type system is powered entirely by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, utilizing its geometric clarity, wide aperture, and modern grotesque warmth to maintain high legibility even with dense tabular data.

### Numerical & Tabular Data
- All currency representations, percentages, transaction ledgers, and portfolio summaries must enable tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums; lining-nums;`) to prevent layout shift during updates and allow seamless side-by-side vertical comparisons between partners.

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- Large figures (e.g., Joint Net Worth) use `display-lg` with tight negative tracking (`-0.03em`) to anchor dashboards with executive authority.
- Micro labels and meta markers (e.g., category badges, assigned partner flags) utilize uppercase styling with generous tracking (`0.04em`) at `label-sm` or `label-md` to maintain effortless visual parsing against dark backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout uses an adaptive fluid grid balanced with clear rhythm tokens to organize dense dual-user financial feeds.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid, `2rem` outer canvas margins, `1.5rem` column gutters. Maximum application container width is constrained to `1280px` for optimal eye scanning across balanced columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Dual-partner split columns stack into synchronized two-column groupings.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Dashboards reflow into focused linear cards, utilizing horizontal carousels for partner breakdown comparisons.

### Spacing Rhythm
- `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px): Used strictly for badge internal padding, micro icon-to-label inline offsets, and dense financial table rows.
- `space-md` (16px): Standard inner card padding and inter-field vertical forms.
- `space-lg` (24px): Primary separation between discrete modules (e.g., Shared Vault vs. Individual Discretionary Budgets).
- `space-xl` (40px): High-level sectional division between dashboard rows.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through tonal stepping paired with emerald atmospheric luminescence, eschewing conventional high-contrast drop shadows.

### Elevation Architecture
1. **Base Floor (`#0B0F17`):** The primary view boundary; non-interactive, deep, and grounded.
2. **Layer 1 (`#111827`):** Structural rails, persistent sidebars, and tab containers. Framed by a subtle bottom/right border of `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.7)`.
3. **Layer 2 (`#182234`):** Cards, insight modules, and transaction groups. Outlined with a continuous 1px border `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.5)`.
4. **Layer 3 (`#1F2D42`):** Dropdowns, modals, contextual popovers, and hovered cards.

### Luminescent Glow & Shadows
- **Card Ambient Halo:** Cards never cast harsh black drop shadows. Instead, elevated status containers utilize an emerald-tinted diffusion: `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -10px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.08)`.
- **Interactive Focus Glow:** Inputs and active cards transition their perimeter border to `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.6)` supported by a direct glow: `0 0 16px -2px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`.
- **Modal Backdrops:** Frosted obsidian glass overlay: `background: rgba(11, 15, 23, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.

## Shapes

The design system implements a **Rounded (Level 2)** shape language, balancing friendly domestic accessibility with the precise geometry expected from modern fintech.

- **Standard Elements (0.5rem / 8px):** Used for inputs, inline transaction rows, utility badges, partner avatar chips, and buttons.
- **Containers & Surfaces (1rem / 16px):** Standard for metrics cards, investment charts, split-settlement dialogs, and overview modules.
- **Modals & Bottom Sheets (1.5rem / 24px):** Applied to major dialogs, couple-onboarding modals, and mobile interactive sheets.
- **Pills / Fully Circular (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for status indicators, live sync indicators, balance tags, and circular user avatars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Shared Impact):** Background `#10B981`, foreground `#0B0F17` (bold typography), radius `0.5rem`. On hover, background deepens to `#059669` accompanied by `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary (Partner Interactive):** Background `#182234`, border `1px solid rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.8)`, foreground `#F9FAFB`. Hover states elevate to `#1F2D42` with border `#10B981`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background, foreground `#94A3B8`. Hover states use foreground `#F9FAFB` and subtle background `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.4)`.

### Cards & Container Modules
- Constructed on `#182234` with `1px solid rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.5)` borders.
- Header zones clearly separate mutual assets from individual partner tallies through subtle internal pill badges or horizontal divider rules (`1px solid rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.8)`).
- Interactive card variants elevate on hover to background `#1F2D42`, shifting the outer border to `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.4)`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Base background `#111827`, 1px border `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.8)`, foreground `#F9FAFB`, placeholder `#64748B`.
- Focus state instantly triggers border color `#10B981` paired with `0 0 0 3px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`.
- Financial amount inputs render numerical figures at `headline-lg` sizing with persistent tabular spacing.

### Checkboxes, Radio Buttons & Switches
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Unchecked states render with `#111827` fill and `rgba(51, 65, 85, 1)` borders. Checked states fill with `#10B981` with pure white `#FFFFFF` indicators.
- **Partner Split Switch (50/50, Custom, Proportional):** Segmented track in `#111827` with `0.25rem` padding; active segmented thumb in `#1F2D42` framed by subtle border and `#10B981` indicator accents.

### Chips & Badges
- **Shared Indicator Chip:** Subtle emerald tint `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, border `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`, text `#10B981`, uppercase `label-sm`.
- **Partner Specific Tags:** Sky tint `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.12)`, border `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.3)`, text `#38BDF8`.

### Shared Financial Primitives
- **Couple Sync Status Pill:** High-visibility pill housing a dual-ring pulsing dot (`#10B981`) validating real-time bank connection balance across both partners.
- **Split Ratio Bar:** Visual progress component displaying proportional contribution (e.g., 60/40 or 50/50) using `#10B981` (Partner A) and `#38BDF8` (Partner B) with an adjustable central tactile scrub handle.
